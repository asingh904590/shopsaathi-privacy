# Delete Your ShopSaathi Account ## How to request account deletion To request deletion of your ShopSaathi account, email **shopsaathi.vyantra@gmail.com** using the email address or phone number associated with your account, if available. **Email subject:** ShopSaathi Account Deletion Request Include your shop ID, if available, and the account contact details needed to identify your account. Do not send your password or one-time verification code. We may contact you to verify that you own or are authorized to manage the account. ## What data may be deleted? After verifying your request, ShopSaathi will review and delete account and associated personal data that can lawfully be deleted. Bills, purchase records, tax-related records, inventory transaction history, and audit records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate business recordkeeping. Any required retention will be limited to the applicable period. Data stored only on your device may not be removed by deleting a cloud account. If you use ShopSaathi on multiple devices, mention this in your request. ## Processing and confirmation We will review your request, ask for clarification if necessary, and confirm the outcome. Processing time depends on verification, the systems involved, and applicable data-retention requirements. ## Request deletion of specific data To ask whether particular data can be deleted without deleting your account, email **shopsaathi.vyantra@gmail.com** with the subject **ShopSaathi Data Deletion Question**. ## Contact **ShopSaathi Support** Email: shopsaathi.vyantra@gmail.com
