# ShopSaathi Privacy Policy **Effective date:** 15 October 2026 ShopSaathi is a retail shop management application that helps merchants manage products, inventory, billing, customer accounts and related business activities. This Privacy Policy explains how ShopSaathi handles information when you use the application. ## 1. Information We Handle Depending on the features you use, ShopSaathi may process: - Shop details and business information. - Product names, prices, stock quantities, purchase records and sales invoices. - Customer and supplier details entered by the merchant. - Account and team-member information used by supported sign-in and membership features. - App settings, synchronization records and technical information required for the application to operate. Please avoid entering unnecessary personal or sensitive information into the app. ## 2. Storage and Synchronization ShopSaathi is designed to support local storage of business records on the user's device. Where cloud synchronization or account-related services are enabled, relevant information may also be processed or stored by the services configured for the application, including Firebase. The information available on a device or in the cloud depends on the features enabled and the user's configuration. Users should maintain appropriate backups of important business records. ## 3. Camera Permission ShopSaathi may request camera access to support barcode scanning. Camera access is used for this feature. Users can manage camera permission through their device settings. ## 4. How Information Is Used Information may be used to: - Generate bills and manage inventory. - Maintain purchase, sales and account records. - Support sign-in, team features and synchronization where available. - Maintain app functionality, troubleshoot errors and improve reliability. - Respond to support and data-related requests. ## 5. Sharing of Information ShopSaathi does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate enabled features, such as authentication and cloud infrastructure. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law. Merchants are responsible for ensuring that they have an appropriate basis and any required permissions to enter and use their customers' and suppliers' information. ## 6. Data Security and Retention Reasonable measures are intended to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Information may remain on a device or in connected services until it is deleted, overwritten, or removed according to the relevant service's retention processes. Removing the app may not automatically delete information previously synchronized to a cloud service. ## 7. Children's Privacy ShopSaathi is intended for retail business management and is not designed specifically for children. ## 8. Your Choices and Data Requests You can manage available device permissions and contact us to ask about access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your use of ShopSaathi.
